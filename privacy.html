Privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Script to Video ("the app") is an internal tool that runs on our team's own computers. This policy explains what it does with Google user data.

What the app accesses

When you connect a YouTube channel, the app asks Google for permission to manage your YouTube account (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.force-ssl). It uses that permission only to:

  • read the name of the channel you connected, so it can show which channel is linked;
  • upload the videos you choose to publish, with their title, description, thumbnail and captions;
  • add those videos to a playlist when you ask it to.

It does not read, change or delete any other videos, comments, subscribers or account information.

Where data is kept

The sign-in token Google issues is stored only on the computer where you connected the channel, in the app's own folder. It is not sent to us or to any server other than Google's. Your videos and projects also stay on that computer until you upload them to YouTube.

Sharing

We do not sell, share or transfer Google user data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising or to train AI models.

Removing access

Disconnect the channel in the app, which revokes the token with Google and deletes it from the computer, or remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Uploads use the YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.
